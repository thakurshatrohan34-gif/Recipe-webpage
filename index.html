Creamy Pasta

This is my favorite pasta recipe. It is easy to cook and tastes really good.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Fresh Cream
  • Cheese
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Heat butter in a pan.
  3. Add garlic.
  4. Mix cream and cheese.
  5. Add pasta and cook for 2 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.

Cooking Terms

Boil
Cook food in hot water.
Saute
Cook with a little oil or butter.
Garnish
Decorate the food before serving.

Tip: Add more cheese if you like a creamy taste.
